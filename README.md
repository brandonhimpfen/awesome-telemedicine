> A curated list of resources for telemedicine platforms, WebRTC clinical video streaming, remote patient monitoring (RPM), store-and-forward systems, and virtual care infrastructure.

Telemedicine technologies enable remote clinical consultations, real-time patient-provider communications, digital diagnostic exchanges, and continuous vital sign tracking across geographic boundaries. This list is intended for digital health software engineers, virtual care architects, clinical informatics leads, and telehealth operators.

## Real-Time Video & Audio Consultation

WebRTC implementations, HIPAA-compliant media servers, and video streaming toolkits optimized for virtual clinical visits.

- [Jitsi Meet Healthcare](https://github.com/jitsi/jitsi-meet) — Open-source, fully encrypted video conferencing platform easily configured for secure telehealth consultations.
- [OpenVidu](https://github.com/OpenVidu/openvidu) — Open-source WebRTC platform providing high-level abstraction APIs for building custom clinical video chat rooms.
- [LiveKit](https://github.com/livekit/livekit) — High-performance, open-source WebRTC media server and SDKs engineered for low-latency multi-party medical consults.
- [Mediasoup](https://github.com/versatica/mediasoup) — Cutting-edge WebRTC Selective Forwarding Unit (SFU) designed for building custom, high-scale telehealth video networks.

## Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)

SDKs, IoT device integration frameworks, and vital sign ingestion pipelines for continuous home care monitoring.

- [OpenICE (Integrated Clinical Environment)](https://github.com/openice) — Open-source platform for medical device interoperability, enabling real-time telemetry streaming from patient monitors.
- [Gattlib](https://github.com/labapart/gattlib) — Library to access Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) health devices such as heart rate monitors, pulse oximeters, and blood pressure cuffs.
- [Vital-Hub](https://github.com/vital-hub) — Open ingestion engine designed for aggregating, normalizing, and storing continuous vital sign data streams from consumer wearables.

## Store-and-Forward (Asynchronous) Systems

Asynchronous consultation software, image exchange pipelines, and secure messaging frameworks for remote diagnostics.

- [DCM4CHEE](https://github.com/dcm4che/dcm4chee-arc-light) — Open-source DICOM archive and image manager supporting asynchronous teleradiology and store-and-forward image transmission.
- [AsyncCare-Engine](https://github.com/asynccare) — Open-source platform enabling structured patient intakes, photo uploads, and asynchronous clinician review workflows.
- [Orthanc PACS](https://github.com/orthanc-server/orthanc) — Lightweight, RESTful DICOM server designed for medical image exchange and web-based teleradiology viewers.

## Virtual Care Platforms & Infrastructure

Full-stack frameworks, virtual waiting rooms, and administrative backends tailored for telehealth providers.

### Open Source Platforms

- [OpenEMR Telehealth Extensions](https://github.com/openemr/openemr) — Open-source electronic health record platform integrated with native video visit capabilities and patient portal messaging.
- [Medplum Telehealth Starter](https://github.com/medplum/medplum) — Developer infrastructure providing FHIR-native APIs, patient scheduling, and virtual visit workflows out of the box.

### Clinical Scheduling & Waiting Rooms

- [Virtual-Waiting-Room](https://github.com/telehealth-ops) — Open-source web component providing virtual queueing, triage chat, and patient waiting experience during digital office hours.
